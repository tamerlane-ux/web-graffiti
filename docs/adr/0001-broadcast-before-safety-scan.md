# Broadcast graffiti before safety scanning

Web Graffiti will show strokes to other users immediately and scan the evolving drawing repeatedly afterward. This preserves a genuinely live creative experience, accepting that unsafe imagery may be briefly visible before detection and removal rather than adding a pre-publication safety buffer.

## Consequences

The production system must support rapid repeated scans, immediate global removal, viewer reporting and hiding, creator rate limits, and an operator emergency freeze. Automated scanning cannot guarantee that unsafe content is never seen.
